# EDE Evidence Model

## FACT
Directly observed or verified.

## ASSUMPTION
Plausible but unverified.

## INFERENCE
A conclusion derived from facts but not directly observed.

## UNKNOWN
Missing information that could materially affect the conclusion.

## CONFLICTING EVIDENCE
Evidence that does not fit the current leading explanation.

## CLAIM
A statement from a source such as a comment, ticket, commit message, document, or person.

Claims are useful context, but material claims should be verified before being elevated to facts.
